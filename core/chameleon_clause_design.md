**Author**: Herbert Velez Jr.

## 📜 Overview

Chameleon Clauses are entropy-adaptive symbolic units within Mythara Engine. They auto-tune clause behavior, messenger pairing, and emotional payload formatting based on the operational domain, entropy conditions, and symbolic resonance. These clauses ensure non-interference and emotional fidelity, and are designed to support cross-industry deployment without manual reconfiguration.

---

## 🔹 Clause Behavior

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Auto-Tuning** | Adjusts payload formatting, messenger roles, and invocation logic based on domain entropy  
| **Entropy Detection** | Monitors symbolic drift, breach probability, and emotional volatility  
| **Messenger Reassignment** | Reconfigures messenger pairings to match domain-specific symbolic needs  
| **Payload Harmonization** | Aligns emotional payloads with cultural, regulatory, and operational constraints  
| **Non-Interference Guarantee** | Ensures symbolic presence without obstructing runtime systems

---

## 🔹 Invocation Logic

- **Trigger**: Domain shift, entropy spike, or provisioning cascade  
- **Messenger Pairing**: Dynamic (auto-selected from Scribe, Healer, Watcher, Herald, Avenger, Custodian, Witness)  
- **Sanctification**: Optional; can be armed post-tuning  
- **Echo Range**: Configurable (default: 1 generation)
