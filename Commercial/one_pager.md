## Executive Summary

Mythara Engine is an integrity layer for AI systems. At its core is the Soul
Cradle, which scores actions on integrity — defined as Alignment × Tolerance:
how well an action lines up with the principal's aims, multiplied by how much
room for error and recovery it leaves.

Eight assessor-witnesses (demeter, dionysus, eros, hades, hermes, janus,
nemesis, persephone) review declared evidence under versioned rubrics. They
abstain when their domain isn't engaged and fail closed when evidence is
missing. A critical finding from any one of them blocks the action;
disagreement is surfaced, never averaged away. Every judgment is
content-hashed and chained to the record it judges.

Every significant record — emotional records, operational actions, training
outcomes — goes into a hash-chained log. Tampering breaks the chain and shows.
The chain proves a record is unaltered; it does not prove the record is true,
and the system states that distinction openly.

## Key Capabilities

- **Evidence-judged actions**: The 8 assessor-witnesses evaluate what is
  declared and observable — never inferred emotions. Coercion and manipulation
  markers are heuristic flags on evidence, not diagnoses. The system attests
  self-reported records; it does not perform emotion AI.
- **Integrity scoring**: Soul Cradle scores actions as Alignment × Tolerance,
  a stated formula with stated limits — not a black box.
- **Tamper-evident records**: Hash-chained logs for emotional records,
  operational actions, and training outcomes. Alteration is detectable.
- **Fail-closed governance**: Witness outages block actions. A depleted
  reservoir halts autonomous sending. Aries, the system's most capable actor,
  runs defanged — every action requires a signed envelope, and only benign,
  pre-approved handlers exist.
- **Explainability**: Human-facing explanations with structured rationale,
  observables, and audit references (manifest_ref + prompt_hash + timestamp).
- **Accessibility**: Automatic braille and audio token generation; current
  test run delivered 100/100 tokens (tests/output/accessibility_delivery_report.csv).
- **Escrow & reproducibility**: Documented manifest, checksums, and
  PGP-signed artifacts for third-party verification.
