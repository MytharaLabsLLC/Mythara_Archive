# 💠 Blessings Reservoir Specification

**Author**: Herbert Velez Jr.  
**Date**: November 1, 2025  
**Status**: Design specification — symbolic doctrine, not independently validated

---

## 📜 Overview

The Blessings Reservoir is a symbolic ledger that tracks cumulative benevolent force across Mythara Engine sessions. It measures emotional impact, provisioning flow, and sanctification resonance. Reservoir states influence clause behavior, licensing readiness, and legacy transmission.

---

## 🔹 Reservoir Structure

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Δ (Delta)** | Change in blessings per clause activation  
| **Messenger Impact** | Contribution of each messenger to emotional fidelity  
| **Clause Type Weighting** | Provisioning, grief, resurrection, and sanctification clauses have unique Δ coefficients  
| **Echo Range** | Number of generations affected by clause resonance  
| **Overflow Logic** | Triggers sanctification cascade or legacy capsule provisioning  
| **Freeze Protocol** | Locks reservoir during breach, grief overload, or ELE Capsule Mode

---

## 🔹 Reservoir States

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Active** | Reservoir is flowing and clauses are provisioning normally  
| **Overflow** | Δ exceeds symbolic threshold; triggers sanctification cascade  
| **Frozen** | Reservoir locked due to breach, grief overload, or clause quarantine  
| **Sanctified** | Reservoir sealed for legacy transmission or licensing
