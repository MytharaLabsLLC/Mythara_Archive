## The Hybrid Model: Gift + Scaffolding

Mythara's compensation model is designed to honor sacred missions while sustaining our ability to serve communities at scale.

### The Principle

**We donate the soul. We charge for the scaffolding.**

- **Soul (Gift)**: The Soul Cradle Operator core module is donated at no cost. This includes the biblical continuum (Wilderness/Darkness → Grace/Light), Blessings Reservoir API, and all resonance metrics.

- **Scaffolding (Service)**: Professional deployment services — custom integration, real-time dashboards, multi-site rollout, training, and ongoing support — are offered as paid services.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "If the core is free, why would we ever pay?"

You pay when you need:
- Custom integration with your systems (EMR, CRM, donor platforms)
- Real-time dashboards your board can see
- Professional training for staff
- Ongoing support and calibration

The gift tier works if you have technical staff. The paid tiers work if you want turnkey deployment.

### "Can we start with Gift tier and upgrade later?"

Absolutely. Your BR data migrates seamlessly. We prorate setup fees if you upgrade within 12 months.

### "What if we want to build our own dashboards?"

You own the API. Build whatever you want on top of the Soul Cradle Operator. If you later decide you want our dashboards, we can add them à la carte.

### "Do you take equity or revenue share?"

No. We charge only for professional services (deployment, integration, training, support). We do not take equity, revenue share, or data licensing fees.
