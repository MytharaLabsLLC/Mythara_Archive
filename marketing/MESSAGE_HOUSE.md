## Positioning

**Accountable AI.** Mythara is the witness layer for artificial intelligence — the system that watches what AI does, records it so it can't be secretly rewritten, and judges it against rubrics in the open.

**The line: "A soul with receipts."**

## The problem (what's true)

AI agents take thousands of actions a day. Nobody — not the vendor, not the buyer, not the auditor — can prove what any of them were. Logs can be edited. "Trust us" is the entire industry's accountability model. It isn't one.

## The product (what's real)

- **8 independent assessor-witnesses.** Every consequential action passes through eight assessors with versioned rubrics over observable evidence. They abstain when their domain isn't engaged. They fail closed when evidence is missing.
- **Dissent is preserved, never averaged.** When the witnesses disagree, the disagreement is published in the record. Averaging dissent is how you manufacture consensus.
- **Hash-chained records.** The witness log, the emotional chain, and the benevolence ledger are each hash-chained. Tamper-evident by construction.
- **Fail-closed.** A critical finding blocks the action. A witness outage halts it. The system does not silently proceed.
- **The Benevolence Reservoir.** Every witnessed deed is ledgered. The pattern of deeds sets the system's latitude — flowing, low, depleted. Shadow benevolence (kindness as cover for extraction) is detected from the pattern, not from any single act.
- **Built in the open.** The repo is public. The rubrics are public. Read the code, run the witnesses, try to fool them.

## The honest contract (the differentiator)

Every AI company sells trust. We sell receipts.

Our chain proves the record is **unaltered**. It never claims the record is **true**. The witnesses judge the evidence they're given; they verify no one's inner state. Intent is inferred from the pattern — labeled as inference, never as verification.

Say it plainly, everywhere: **unaltered, not true.** That distinction is the whole product, and nobody else will say it.

## Voice

Direct. Human. Sharp. Short sentences. Conviction.

- Write like a person who believes the thing, not a brand performing belief.
- Show, don't hype. Specifics beat adjectives.
- It's fine to be blunt. It's not fine to be cruel.

**Never:** mail-merge pleasantries ("Best regards," "I hope this finds you well"), "leverage," "synergy," "cutting-edge," "revolutionize," "game-changer," "unlock," "supercharge," fake urgency ("only 3 slots"), fake scarcity, or any sentence that could have been written by any company about any product.

## Forbidden claims

Do not claim, imply, or let a reader infer:

- Customers, deployments, pilots, or "trusted by"
- Metrics ("0 critical leaks," determinism percentages, hours saved)
- Certifications (SOC 2, HIPAA, FDA — readiness work is not certification)
- ROI numbers or payback math
- Pricing (no confirmed public price — do not use $60K/year)
- "Emotion AI" — never. The chain attests records; it does not infer emotions.
- Proof of feelings, truth, abuse, or gaslighting — the honest contract forbids it
- Production-readiness — say "building in the open" instead
- Being "first" or "only" — say "we looked; show us who else" instead
