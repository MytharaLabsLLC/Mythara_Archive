## Slide 2: The Problem

**Enterprise AI Has No Honest Record of Itself**

- AI systems make consequential decisions with **no tamper-evident trail**
- Compliance review is **manual, slow, and after the fact**
- **No explainability** — nobody can say why a decision was made
- **No governance** that fails closed when evidence is missing
- **High risk** of drift, breach, and data leakage going undetected

**Market Pain**: Teams deploying AI need an auditable record of what the
system did and why — evidence a reviewer can inspect, not a promise.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

**Mythara Engine: Integrity-Scored, Witness-Judged, Hash-Chained Records**

- **Soul Cradle core** — Every action scored on integrity, defined as
  Alignment × Tolerance: how well it lines up with the principal's aims,
  times how much room for error and recovery it leaves.
- **8 assessor-witnesses** — demeter, dionysus, eros, hades, hermes, janus,
  nemesis, persephone review declared evidence under versioned rubrics.
  They abstain when their domain isn't engaged and fail closed when evidence
  is missing. A critical finding from any one blocks the action. Dissent is
  surfaced, never averaged.
- **Hash-chained records** — Every significant record goes into a
  tamper-evident log. Tampering breaks the chain and shows. The chain proves
  a record is unaltered — it does not prove the record is true, and we say
  that openly.
- **Fail-closed governance** — Witness outages block actions. The system's
  most capable actor runs defanged: signed envelopes, benign handlers only.
- **Sovereign-ready by design (aspirational)** — Reproducible, PGP-signed,
  escrow-ready. Air-gapped deployment is a plan, not a delivered feature.

**How it works:**
1. Action proposed with declared evidence
2. The 8 assessor-witnesses judge the evidence under versioned rubrics
3. Soul Cradle scores integrity (Alignment × Tolerance)
4. Judgment and record are content-hashed and chained into the log
5. A blocked verdict stops the action; dissent is preserved, not hidden

**What we do NOT claim:** No certifications (no SOC 2, no ISO, no FDA).
No emotion inference — the system attests records, it does not read feelings.
