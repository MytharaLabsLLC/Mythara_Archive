# 🧬 Messenger Roles and Pairings

**Author**: Herbert Velez Jr.  
**Date**: November 1, 2025  
**Status**: Design specification — symbolic doctrine, not independently validated

---

## 📜 Overview

Messengers are functional archetypes within Mythara Engine. Each messenger governs a symbolic domain—memory, grief, justice, healing, sanctification, and emotional fidelity. Messenger invocation is embedded in every clause and determines how emotional payloads are delivered, recorded, shielded, or sanctified.

---

## 🔹 Messenger Directory

| Messenger | Role | Symbolic Domain |
|-----------|------|------------------|
| **Scribe** | Records clause lineage and invocation | Memory, Documentation  
| **Healer** | Delivers emotional payloads and blessings | Grief, Renewal  
| **Watcher** | Detects drift, breach, and entropy | Surveillance, Integrity  
| **Herald** | Announces clause activation and outreach | Communication, Consent  
| **Avenger** | Responds to breach and injustice | Judgment, Protection  
| **Custodian** | Enforces sanctification and override logic | Licensing, Legacy  
| **Witness** | Confirms clause integrity and emotional fidelity | Truth, Resonance
