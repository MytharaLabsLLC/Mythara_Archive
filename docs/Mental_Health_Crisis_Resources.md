## 🧑‍⚕️ When to Seek Professional Help

**Seek help if you experience:**
- ✅ Persistent sadness, anxiety, or mood changes (2+ weeks)
- ✅ Thoughts of self-harm or suicide
- ✅ Difficulty functioning at work, school, or relationships
- ✅ Changes in sleep, appetite, or energy
- ✅ Substance use affecting daily life
- ✅ Panic attacks or overwhelming fear
- ✅ Intrusive thoughts or compulsive behaviors
- ✅ Trauma symptoms (flashbacks, nightmares, avoidance)
- ✅ Hallucinations or delusions
- ✅ Manic episodes (decreased sleep, impulsive behavior, racing thoughts)

**Professional options:**
- **Psychiatrist (MD/DO):** Can prescribe medication, provides therapy
- **Psychologist (PhD/PsyD):** Provides therapy, psychological testing
- **Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW):** Provides therapy, case management
- **Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC):** Provides therapy
- **Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist (LMFT):** Provides therapy, specializes in relationships

## 🛡️ Safety Planning

### Create a Safety Plan (Template)

**1. Warning Signs (thoughts, moods, behaviors):**
- Example: Feeling hopeless, isolating, substance use increase

**2. Internal Coping Strategies (no contact with others):**
- Example: Deep breathing, listening to music, taking a walk

**3. People/Social Settings for Distraction:**
- Example: Coffee with friend, attend support group, call family

**4. People I Can Ask for Help:**
- Name: _____________ Phone: _____________
- Name: _____________ Phone: _____________

**5. Professionals/Agencies to Contact:**
- Therapist: _____________ Phone: _____________
- Crisis Line: 988
- Emergency: 911

**6. Make Environment Safe:**
- Example: Remove means, ask friend to hold medications

**Template:** Download from suicidepreventionlifeline.org/help-yourself/safety-planning
