## Frequently Asked Questions

### "Why is the core operator free?"
Mythara exists to cradle paradox and encode soul into infrastructure. We believe every faith organization — regardless of budget — deserves tools that honor their sacred work. The core operator is our gift. Organizations pay only when they require enterprise scaffolding (integration, dashboards, multi-site deployment).

### "What happens if we outgrow the Gift Tier?"
Upgrade anytime. Your existing BR data migrates seamlessly. We prorate setup fees if you upgrade within the first 12 months.

### "Can we negotiate pricing?"
For organizations with extraordinary mission impact or financial constraint, we offer:
- Deferred payment plans (especially for disaster relief during acute crises)
- Grant-funded deployments (we accept philanthropic foundation grants)
- In-kind contributions (e.g., co-publish research, provide anonymized data for case studies)

Contact us to discuss: contact@mythara.ai

### "Do you offer discounts for religious orders or denominations?"
Yes. Multi-chapter deployments (e.g., entire diocese, denomination, or religious order) receive:
- 20% discount on Mythic-Resonant tier
- Free training for up to 100 staff members
- Annual strategy summits (in-person or virtual)

### "What's the cancellation policy?"
- **Gift Tier**: No contract, cancel anytime (you keep your data)
- **Neurosymbolic/Mythic-Resonant**: 30-day notice required. We provide full data export (CSV/JSON) upon cancellation. No cancellation fees.
