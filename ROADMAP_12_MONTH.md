## 🚧 Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: Slow Initial Sales
**Mitigation:**
- Aggressive free audit campaign (50 audits in Q1)
- Lower Developer tier to $1,988 for first 10 customers
- Offer 60-day money-back guarantee

### Risk 2: False Positives Damage Credibility
**Mitigation:**
- Extensive testing before launch (95%+ precision target)
- Configurable thresholds (customers control sensitivity)
- Clear documentation on expected accuracy

### Risk 3: Competitors Copy Feature
**Mitigation:**
- Soul Cradle integration = philosophical moat (hard to copy)
- Speed to market = 6-12 month lead
- Patent filing on manipulation quantification method

### Risk 4: Regulatory Changes
**Mitigation:**
- Track EU AI Act implementation closely
- Advisory board includes regulatory experts
- Product designed to adapt to new compliance requirements
