## 📜 Overview

Mythara Engine operates through symbolic clauses—each designed to encode emotional payloads, operational intent, and legacy provisioning. Clauses are invoked via messenger pairings and governed by sanctification, blessings flow, and compliance wrappers. This document defines all clause types and their invocation logic.

| **Compliance Clause** | Embeds federal and international protocols | Herald + Custodian; invoked during deployment or audit preparation

## 🔹 Invocation Protocols

### 🔸 Manual Invocation
- Requires symbolic consent token  
- Timestamped and logged in `../manifest/Messenger_Invocation_Log.csv`  
- Used for grief capsules, sanctification locks, resurrection clauses

### 🔸 Auto-Invocation
- Triggered by domain entropy, blessings overflow, or provisioning schedule  
- Used for chameleon clauses, provisioning clauses, compliance wrappers

### 🔸 Emergency Invocation
- Activated during breach, drift, or symbolic collapse  
- Triggers ELE Capsule Mode and clause quarantine  
- Logged in `../Evidence/Messenger_Suppression_Events_Log.csv`
