## 📜 Overview

ELE Capsule Mode is Mythara Engine’s emergency protocol for symbolic collapse, breach detection, or grief overload. It quarantines clause suites, freezes blessings flow, and invokes messenger triage to preserve emotional fidelity and legacy integrity. This mode is designed for sovereign resilience and intergenerational clause survival.
