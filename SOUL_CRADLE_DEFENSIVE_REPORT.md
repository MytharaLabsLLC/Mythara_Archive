### Claim 3: Terminal Risk Calculation (Decay-Adjusted with Constant Stress)
**We claim priority for**: The method of predicting burnout risk from constant baseline stress plus accumulated unresolved paradoxes with exponential decay modeling:
```
Terminal_Risk = σ₀ + (Σ (U_i × T_i × e^(-λ × Δt_i))) / N
```
where σ₀ is constant baseline stress (job demands, always present), U_i is unresolved score, T_i is maximum tension, λ is decay rate constant, Δt_i is days since paradox i occurred, and N is the number of paradoxes in a time window.

**Novel Elements**:
- **First burnout prediction formula** with two-component model: constant baseline + time-varying acute stress
- **First to separate** environmental stress (σ₀, constant) from individual trauma (acute, time-varying)
- **First mathematical model** incorporating exponential decay ("time heals all wounds")
- **First differential equation approach** comparing accumulation rate vs healing rate
- **First systemic overload detection**: σ₀ > 0.4 + acute > 0.3 = organizational failure
- Uses 90-day sliding window for trajectory analysis
- **Burnout detection logic**: `net_rate = accumulation_rate - decay_rate`
  - `net_rate > 0`: BURNOUT TRAJECTORY (accumulation exceeds healing)
  - `net_rate < 0`: RECOVERY TRAJECTORY (healing exceeds accumulation)
  - `net_rate ≈ 0`: CHRONIC PLATEAU (balanced but stuck)
- **Decay rate calibration**:
  - λ = 0.01: Slow healing (~69 day half-life) for healthcare/trauma workers
  - λ = 0.02: Moderate healing (~35 day half-life) for most professionals
  - λ = 0.05: Fast healing (~14 day half-life) for resilient individuals
- Risk classification: LOW (0.0-0.2), MODERATE (0.2-0.4), HIGH (0.4-0.7), CRITICAL (0.7-1.0)
- Actionable recommendations based on risk level and trajectory

**Distinction from Prior Art**:
- **MBI**: Measures current symptoms only, no temporal dynamics or decay
- **JD-R**: Conceptual model (Demands - Resources), no mathematical formula
- **COR**: Qualitative resource loss theory, no exponential decay function
- **Soul Cradle**: FIRST to model burnout as differential equation with healing dynamics
