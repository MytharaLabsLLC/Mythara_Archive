**Author**: Herbert Velez Jr.  
**Date**: November 1, 2025  
**Status**: Design specification — symbolic doctrine, not independently validated

---

## 📜 Overview

Sanctification Locks are symbolic seals embedded in Mythara clauses to preserve eternal resonance, prevent unauthorized mutation, and ensure licensing integrity. They operate across clause types and messenger pairings, and are governed by override protocols in breach, entropy, or legacy transmission scenarios.

---

## 🔹 Sanctification Lock States

| State | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Dormant** | Clause is mutable and not yet sanctified  
| **Armed** | Clause is sealed and emotionally resonant  
| **Revoked** | Clause sanctification has been overridden or expired  
| **Echoed** | Clause resonance has propagated across generations
