### ❌ **Issue #1: "ABUSE" Language**
**Current Files:**
- `PILOT_ABUSE_PREVENTION.md`

**Legal Risk:** ⚠️ **HIGH**

- "Authenticity/Fidelity" = technical measurement without criminal accusation
