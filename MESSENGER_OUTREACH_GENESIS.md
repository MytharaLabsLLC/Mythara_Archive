## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Send Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am their timezone** (highest open rates)
2. **Keep message under 100 words** (attention span)
3. **One CTA only**: "Would a pilot be valuable?" (not multiple asks)
4. **Personalize first line** (mention recent news about their company)
5. **Follow up in 3 days** (80% of deals need 5+ touches)
6. **If they respond with questions**: Offer to send the pilot package immediately
7. **If they ghost**: Try a different person at same company in 2 weeks
