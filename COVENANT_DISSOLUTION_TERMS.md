## 🔄 Exchanges Only

We offer **exchanges to products of equal or lesser value** under the following conditions:

### Eligible Exchanges

**1. Pilot to Enterprise (Upgrade)**
- Original purchase: 7-day Pilot ($49)
- Exchange to: Enterprise tier (any level)
- **Credit applied:** Full $49 credited toward Enterprise purchase
- Example: Foundation tier ($25,000) - $49 pilot = $24,951 due

**2. Enterprise Tier to Enterprise Tier (Lateral/Downgrade)**
- Original purchase: Enterprise Professional ($40K/year)
- Exchange to: Enterprise Foundation ($25K/year) 
- **Credit applied:** Prorated remaining time on current plan
- Example: 6 months left on $40K plan = $20K credit → Applied to $25K plan

**3. Pilot to Different Pilot (Technical Issues Only)**
- Original: 7-day hosted pilot
- Issue: Technical incompatibility, hosting platform issues
- Exchange to: Self-hosted pilot deployment assistance
- **Credit applied:** Full $49 (no additional cost)

### Ineligible Exchanges

❌ **Cannot exchange for higher value without payment:**
- Cannot upgrade from Foundation ($25K) to Professional ($40K) without paying difference

❌ **Cannot exchange after expiration:**
- 7-day pilot expired? Must purchase fresh pilot or upgrade to Enterprise

❌ **Cannot exchange for refund:**
- No exchanges for cash refunds under any circumstances

❌ **Cannot exchange after abuse violations:**
- Accounts terminated for TOS violations forfeit all exchange rights
