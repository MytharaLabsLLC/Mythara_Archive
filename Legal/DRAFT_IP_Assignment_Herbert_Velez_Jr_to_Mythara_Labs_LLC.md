### 1. Parties

**Assignor:** Herbert Velez Jr., an individual residing in Denver, Colorado

Assignor's right, title, and interest in the following (the "Assigned IP"):

1. **All Mythara source code** — every file in the GitHub repository listed
   in Schedule A, including all branches and history, plus any Mythara code
   held outside that repository. **[CONFIRM: list any other repositories,

3. **SERE training materials** — the SERE course, the Slime system, the
   Crucible exam, adversary simulations, and all related documentation. SERE
   is a training simulation; nothing assigned includes any operational
   offensive capability.
4. **Assessor and witness systems** — the 8 assessor-witnesses (demeter,
   dionysus, eros, hades, hermes, janus, nemesis, persephone), the witnessing
   engine, and related rubrics and documentation.
5. **Documentation** — all technical, legal, and commercial documents
   describing the systems above.
6. **Brand assets** — the names "Mythara," "Mythara Engine," "Soul Cradle,"
   and related marks, to the extent Assignor owns rights in them (trademark

This includes all copyrights, trade secrets, and related rights in the
Assigned IP, and the right to sue for past infringement. **[CONFIRM: patent
