## ⚠️ CRITICAL LEGAL ISSUES IDENTIFIED & REMEDIATED

### **Issue #1: Criminal Law Terminology (REMEDIATED)**
**Problem:** Product named "Emotional Extortion Detector"  
**Risk:** "Extortion" is felony under 18 USC § 871-876; creates liability if system misidentifies legal communications as criminal acts  
**Solution:** Renamed to "Will Integrity Guardian" with focus on "authenticity assessment" vs. "crime detection"  
**Attorney Action:** Verify new terminology doesn't trigger different regulatory framework

---

### **Issue #2: FDA Medical Device Risk (REMEDIATED)**
**Problem:** Product named "Dr. Mythara Medical Team Suite"  
**Risk:** "Dr" prefix + "Medical" triggers FDA 21 CFR Part 11 oversight  
**Solution:** Renamed to "Mythara Wellness Guardian Suite"  
**Attorney Action:** Confirm "wellness" categorization vs. "medical device"; review FDA guidance on AI health tools

---

### **Issue #3: Implied Warranty Exposure (PARTIALLY REMEDIATED)**
**Problem:** Files named "*_COMPLETE.md" and repeated "compliance" promises  
**Risk:** "Complete" implies warranty of fitness; "compliance" creates implied warranty that regulations will be satisfied  
**Solution:** Removed "_COMPLETE" suffix; replaced "compliance" with "covenant integrity" and "governance framework"  
**Attorney Action:** Review whether new terminology still creates implied warranties; strengthen disclaimers if needed

---

### **Issue #4: Consumer Protection - "Abuse" Framing (REMEDIATED)**
**Problem:** "Pilot Abuse Prevention" policy  
**Risk:** "Abuse" language creates adversarial relationship; inconsistent enforcement could trigger consumer protection complaints  
**Solution:** Renamed to "Genesis Covenant Safeguards" with service-oriented language  
**Attorney Action:** Review enforcement procedures for FTC compliance; ensure non-discriminatory application

---

### **Issue #5: Trademark Dilution (REMEDIATED)**
**Problem:** Generic terms ("Pilot," "Compliance," "Medical") not trademarkable  
**Risk:** Brand identity weak; competitors can use same terminology  
**Solution:** Created unique symbolic vocabulary ("Genesis Covenant," "Will Integrity Guardian," "Sanctification Key")  
**Attorney Action:** **Conduct trademark clearance search**; advise on registration strategy

---

### **Issue #6: Licensing Tier Confusion (REQUIRES REVIEW)**
**Problem:** "Governance License" vs. "Global Governance Framework" (product vs. contract unclear)  
**Risk:** Customer confusion about what they're purchasing  
**Solution:** Separated product name (Universal Governance Lattice) from contract name (Enterprise Covenant Agreement)  
**Attorney Action:** **Review for contract formation clarity**; ensure consideration is clear

---

### **Issue #7: Indemnification Scope (REQUIRES REVIEW)**
**Background:** November 2025 license tiers offered $10M-$25M indemnification. **Those offers were removed in the September 2026 honest-tree pass — no indemnification is currently offered and no insurance backs the software** (see `LICENSE.md`, "No warranties, no indemnification").  
**Risk:** Any indemnification offered without insurance backing creates uncapped exposure; unclear what would trigger coverage  
**Solution:** NOT YET ADDRESSED - requires attorney guidance on whether any indemnification should be offered at all  
**Attorney Action:** **Advise whether to offer indemnification at all**; if yes, draft clause with clear scope, exclusions, and caps, and require insurance backing to be in place before any such offer is made
