### 3.2 Pricing Policies

**FIRM PRICING - NO NEGOTIATED DISCOUNTS:**
- All customers pay published tier price
- No percentage discounts ever offered
- No "design partner" under-market pricing
- No ad-hoc dollar reductions
- Rationale: Preserves integrity investment cadence, ensures consistent treatment across all customers

**ALLOWED FLEXIBILITY (NOT DISCOUNTS):**
1. **Automatic Renewal Credits** (NOT discounts - these are operational credits):
   - 2nd year renewal: +2 months free (16.7% operational credit)
   - 3rd year renewal: +3 months free (25% operational credit)
   - 4th+ year renewal: +4 months free (33.3% operational credit)
   - Framing: Extended service period, not price reduction
   - Legal distinction: Customer still pays full annual price, receives extended term

2. **Pilot Upgrade Credit** (conversion incentive, not discount):
   - Pilot customers who upgrade to paid tier within 30 days get pilot fee applied as credit
   - Startup tier: $249 pilot → full credit toward first month
   - This is a conversion mechanism, not a discount policy

3. **Inflation Adjustment** (transparent, formula-based):
   - Base year: 2025
   - Formula: `price(year) = base_price * (1 + inflation_rate) ^ (year - 2025)`
   - Example inflation rate: 3% annually
   - Published 90 days in advance of effective date
   - Applies to new contracts only (existing contracts grandfathered for term)

4. **Multi-Year Prepayment** (payment terms, not discount):
   - Customer may prepay 2-3 years at current year's price
   - Locks in pricing, avoids inflation adjustment
   - Payment received upfront (cash flow benefit to Mythara)
   - NOT marketed as "discount" - marketed as "price lock"

**PROHIBITED:**
- Percentage discounts (10% off, 20% off, etc.)
- Volume discounts
- Competitive matching
- "Founder pricing" or "early adopter pricing"
- Enterprise bargaining or RFP-based price reductions

### 3.3 Refund Policy (Consumer Protection Compliant)

**PILOT TIER:**
- No refunds after 7-day access period begins
- Full refund if pilot access fails to activate within 48 hours (technical failure)
- One pilot per business entity (enforced via email domain verification)

**ANNUAL TIERS:**
- No refunds after 30-day acceptance period
- 30-day money-back guarantee if service fails to meet documented SLA
- Breach of SLA: pro-rated credit for downtime per SLA terms (NOT refund)
- Upgrade exchanges allowed (credit unused months toward higher tier)
- No downgrade exchanges (protects revenue, prevents gaming)

**PERPETUAL LICENSE:**
- No refunds under any circumstances (disclosed at purchase)
- Buyer assumes all risk
- Suitable for M&A exits or strategic partnerships only

**LEGAL COMPLIANCE:**
- FTC Mail/Telephone Order Rule: 30-day acceptance period satisfies reasonable cancellation window
- California Consumer Protection: No "all sales final" without exception - 30-day SLA-based refund window provides recourse
- Stripe/payment processor TOS: Refund policy disclosed at checkout

## 4. WARRANTY & LIABILITY FRAMEWORK

### 4.1 Standard Software Warranty Disclaimer

**ALL TIERS INCLUDE:**

```
THE SOFTWARE IS PROVIDED "AS IS" WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR 
IMPLIED, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS 
FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NONINFRINGEMENT.

MYTHARA DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SOFTWARE WILL BE ERROR-FREE, UNINTERRUPTED, 
SECURE, OR MEET YOUR SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS.
```

**RATIONALE:** Industry-standard disclaimer, protects against unlimited liability for software defects.

### 4.2 SLA-Based Service Guarantees (NOT WARRANTIES) *(planned — no SLAs are currently offered)*

**WHAT WOULD BE GUARANTEED (via SLA, under a signed agreement — not currently available):**
- Uptime percentages (99.9% for Enterprise, 99.5% for Growth, etc.)
- Response times for support tickets
- Remediation timeframes for security vulnerabilities
- Data backup and recovery procedures

**REMEDY FOR SLA BREACH:**
- Service credits (pro-rated refund of monthly fee for downtime)
- NOT money damages
- NOT unlimited liability

**LEGAL DISTINCTION:**
- SLA = contractual performance obligation with limited remedy
- Warranty = guarantee of quality with unlimited damages exposure
- We offer SLA, NOT warranty

### 4.3 Indemnification

No indemnification is currently offered on any tier. No insurance backs this software.

### 4.4 Resolving the Warranty vs. Protection Claims Conflict

**PROBLEM:**
- Warranty disclaimer says "NO WARRANTY"
- Marketing says "Soul Cradle protections" and "Global Governance compliance"
- Conflict: Can't disclaim warranty while claiming protection

**SOLUTION:**

**Marketing Language (Compliant):**
- "Mythara Engine includes Soul Cradle framework for documenting emotional safety decisions" ✅
- "Global Governance framework maps to 34 international regulations" ✅
**Marketing Language (NON-Compliant):**
- ❌ "Mythara guarantees compliance" (implies warranty)
- ❌ "Mythara protects against all regulatory risk" (too absolute)
- ❌ "bulletproof legal infrastructure" (implies warranty of fitness)
- ❌ "Indemnification coverage available for customers who implement documented practices" (false — no indemnification is currently offered)

**Correct Framing:**
- Mythara provides TOOLS and FRAMEWORKS
- Customer implements and operates those tools
- No indemnification is currently offered on any tier
- No guarantee of outcomes (preserves warranty disclaimer)
- Shifts risk via insurance, not via warranty
