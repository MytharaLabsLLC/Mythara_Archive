## 07 [LI]

The most important design decision we made wasn't technical. It was philosophical:

When our assessor-witnesses disagree, the dissent goes into the permanent record. We don't average it. We don't smooth it. We publish who disagreed and why.

Why? Because disagreement is usually the signal, not the noise. The witness who flagged the problem saw something the others missed. Average that away and you've deleted the most informative part of the review — and quietly trained every future witness to stay silent.

If you run any review process — AI oversight, code review, incident postmortems — try it: give the minority its own paragraph. Watch what happens to the quality of the majority opinion.
