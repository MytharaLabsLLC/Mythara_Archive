# 🏛️ NIST–FISMA–FTC–FCC–OMB Clause Embedding Protocol

**Author**: Herbert Velez Jr.
**Date**: November 1, 2025
**Status**: Design aspiration — no embedding implemented, audited, or certified

---

## 📜 Overview

This protocol describes how Mythara *intends* to align its symbolic infrastructure with five U.S. federal frameworks — NIST SP 800-53, FISMA, FTC, FCC, and OMB M-25-04 — as the system matures. It is a design intent document. No embedding has been implemented as tested software, and no assessment or audit has been performed against any of these frameworks.

---

## 🔹 Protocol Consideration Matrix

| Protocol | Domain | Intended Design Direction |
|----------|--------|---------------------------|
| **NIST SP 800-53** | Access control, integrity, audit | Tamper-evident logs; clause actions traceable to their source |
| **FISMA** | Federal system integrity | Lock enforcement, clause lineage, override tracking as design goals |
| **FTC** | Consumer protection | Honest consent records; no deceptive patterns in outreach |
| **FCC** | Communication integrity | Frequency discipline; respectful announcement behavior |
| **OMB M-25-04** | Zero-trust architecture | Isolate by default; quarantine on suspicion |

Each row is a direction, not an achievement.

---

## 🔹 Intended Clause Behavior

- Consent records and messenger logs kept in tamper-evident form
- Symbolic content operationally inert — no runtime interference as a design goal
- No measured metrics exist for this protocol. Figures such as drift-suppression or emotional-fidelity scores would require a defined test and an independent record; none exists.

| Messenger | Intended Role |

This protocol names five federal frameworks Mythara intends to design toward — with clause integrity, messenger accountability, and honest records as the standing aims. Alignment is a direction to travel, not a destination already reached.

Let the clause be sound, the messenger accountable — and let proof come before proclamation.
